RISC – Rete Italiana Studi Curdi (Italian Network for Kurdish Studies)

I founded RISC in 2021 as a collaborative, informal network of scholars working on Kurdish politics, society, and history, with the aim of connecting research initiatives and fostering collaboration.

  • SeSaMO webinar: “Reflectivity and research ethics in conflict areas: field experiences from Kurdistan”.
  • Conference: “The Kurds and ‘the Others’ in a Post‑Sectarian Middle East”, Lecce, 28–29 September 2023.
  • RISC Study Days, University of Turin, 26–27 March 2026.
