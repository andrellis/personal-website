Le relazioni esterne del DAANES nell’interregno siriano

Publication

Novellis, Andrea. 2025. “Le relazioni esterne del DAANES nell’interregno siriano.” In La guerra civile siriana. Rome: Carocci.

Examines how the DAANES uses external relations to seek legitimacy, support and negotiating influence without recognition as a state. The chapter considers how Assad’s fall and changing regional relationships require the administration to reassess its diplomatic priorities and strategies for preserving autonomy.
