Paradigm Lock-In and Strategic Surprise: Explaining Israel’s October 7 Intelligence Failure

Publication

Hanau Santini, Ruth, and Andrea Novellis. 2026. “Paradigm Lock-In and Strategic Surprise: Explaining Israel’s October 7 Intelligence Failure.” Middle East Policy 33(4).

Explains how political assumptions about Hamas became embedded in Israeli intelligence routines, leading analysts to discount credible warnings before October 7. The article traces how institutionalised interpretations of an adversary can prevent recognition of changes in its strategy.
