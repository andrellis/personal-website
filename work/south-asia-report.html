Humanitarianism, Development Assistance, Early Warning and Political Violence in South Asia in a Post-Liberal International Order

Publication

Hanau Santini, Ruth, Andrea Novellis, Rajni Gamage, and Devyani Chaturvedi, eds. 2026. Humanitarianism, Development Assistance, Early Warning and Political Violence in South Asia in a Post-Liberal International Order. ISAS Special Report 37. Institute of South Asian Studies, National University of Singapore, and University of Naples “L’Orientale”.

This edited report examines humanitarian action and early warning amid overlapping crises and the politicisation of aid in South Asia. It includes Andrea Novellis’s chapter, “Early Warning and Sri Lanka’s Economic Crisis,” on international organisations’ failure to recognise the crisis despite observable warning signs.
