Earning, Allocating, Providing: The Financial and Budget Management of the Democratic Autonomous Administration of North and East Syria

Publication

Corradi, Edoardo, and Andrea Novellis. 2026. “Earning, Allocating, Providing: The Financial and Budget Management of the Democratic Autonomous Administration of North and East Syria.” Civil Wars, 1–35. Published online 8 September.

Reconstructs the DAANES’s revenue sources and spending priorities to examine how rebel coalitions finance governance. It argues that budget allocation can sustain coalition cohesion, but that centralised control over strategic resources and informal patronage can undermine formal financial power-sharing.
