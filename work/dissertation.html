Why Fight When You Can Rule Together? Rebel Power-Sharing During Civil Wars

Publication

Novellis, Andrea. 2024. Why Fight When You Can Rule Together? Rebel Power-Sharing During Civil Wars. PhD dissertation, University of Milan.

Investigates why rebel organisations establish power-sharing institutions during civil wars. Comparing cases in Nepal, Sri Lanka and northeast Syria, the dissertation examines how ideology, alliances and relations with local constituencies shape the development of shared wartime governance.
