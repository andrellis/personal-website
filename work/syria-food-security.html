Syria: Food Security and Stability in the Post-Assad Transition

Publication

Novellis, Andrea. 2026. “Syria: Food Security and Stability in the Post-Assad Transition.” In Food Sovereignty and Food Security: The Increasing Weaponisation of Food, edited by Ruth Hanau Santini, 105–124. Naples: UniorPress.

Examines how control over wheat procurement, bread distribution and water shapes political authority in Syria’s post-Assad transition. The chapter connects food insecurity to administrative fragmentation and environmental pressures, identifying risks when existing food-governance arrangements are dismantled before replacements can function.
