The Divided Self: How Ideological Compartmentalization Drives Rebel-to-Party Success in Sri Lanka

Publication

Novellis, Andrea. 2026. “The Divided Self: How Ideological Compartmentalization Drives Rebel-to-Party Success in Sri Lanka.” Democratization, 1–27.

Analyses the JVP’s public communications between 2015 and 2024 to explain its electoral adaptation after insurgent defeat. It develops the concept of ideological compartmentalization: separating a protected ideological core from a flexible electoral front, allowing broad appeals without requiring linear ideological moderation.
